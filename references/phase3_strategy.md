# Phase 3: Strategy & Competitive Analysis

## Objective
Assess whether the company has a winning formula. Does the market provide tailwinds?
Does the company hold sustainable advantages over competitors?
Strong financials mean nothing if strategic positioning is weak.

## Researcher: Information Gathering

### Items to Search

**Market Dynamics**
- TAM (Total Addressable Market) size and growth rate
- Structural changes (technology shifts, regulatory changes, consumer behavior)
- Adjacent markets in the value chain

**Corporate Strategy**
- Mid-term management plan and progress
- Product portfolio (core offerings + new initiatives)
- STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning)
- Recent capex, M&A, R&D investment direction

**Competitive Advantage**
- Main competitors and market share
- Differentiation at the product/channel level
- Technical advantages (patents, proprietary tech, know-how)
- Switching costs, network effects, economies of scale

### Recommended Search Queries
- `"{company}" mid-term plan 2025` / `"{company}" 中期経営計画`
- `"{company}" market share competitors`
- `"{industry}" market size growth forecast`
- `"{company}" capex M&A investment`
- `"{ticker}" competitive advantage moat`

### Priority Sources
- Mid-term plan documents (company IR page)
- Industry reports (Yano Research, Fuji Chimera, Gartner, IDC)
- Earnings call Q&A sessions

## Analyst: Evaluation

### Market Attractiveness
- Is TAM expanding? (annual growth rate?)
- Market maturity stage (nascent / growth / mature / declining)
- Do structural changes benefit or threaten the company?

### Strategy Assessment
- Are mid-term plan targets realistic (not too aggressive, not too conservative)?
- Does investment direction align with market trends?
- Portfolio balance: core business dependency vs. new business development

### Competitor Benchmarking
Compare against 2-3 main competitors:
- Revenue scale, growth rate
- Margins
- Core KPIs (identified in Phase 2)
- Strategic direction differences
