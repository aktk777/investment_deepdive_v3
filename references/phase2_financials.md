# Phase 2: Financial Statement Analysis

## Objective
Verify the company's earning power and financial health with hard numbers.
Use actual financial data to support or refute the hypothesis formed in Phase 1.

## Researcher: Information Gathering

### Items to Search

**Growth**
- Revenue 3-5 year trend and CAGR
- Operating profit trend and operating margin
- EBITDA and EBITDA margin (if available)

**Cash Flow**
- Operating CF, Investing CF, Free CF trends
- Operating CF > Net Income? (earnings quality check)

**Revenue Structure**
- Segment-level revenue and profit
- Geographic revenue mix
- Revenue concentration in key products/services

**Cost Structure**
- COGS ratio, SGA ratio trends
- Breakdown: raw materials, labor, depreciation (from annual report / 有価証券報告書)

**Forward-Looking Indicators**
- Company guidance (profit/revenue forecasts)
- Order backlog (for B2B companies)
- ARR/MRR (for SaaS companies)

**KPI Identification**
- Identify the core KPIs driving this company's growth
  (e.g., SaaS → ARR, NRR, churn rate / EC → GMV, take rate / Manufacturing → utilization, ASP)
- KPI trends (quarterly if possible)
- Benchmark KPIs against competitors

### Recommended Search Queries
- `"{company}" 決算 売上 営業利益 推移` / `"{ticker}" revenue operating income trend`
- `"{company}" segment revenue breakdown`
- `"{ticker}" cash flow statement`
- `"{ticker}" earnings call transcript`

### Priority Sources
- Earnings summaries & presentation materials (company IR page)
- Annual securities reports (EDINET: disclosure2.edinet-fsa.go.jp) for JP stocks
- SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q) for US stocks
- User-attached earnings PDFs take highest priority

## Analyst: Evaluation

### Growth Quality
- Revenue growth trend: accelerating / decelerating / stable?
- Is profit growth outpacing revenue growth? (operating leverage)
- Growth quality: organic vs. M&A-driven

### Cash Flow Health
- Is operating CF consistently positive?
- FCF positive, or temporarily negative due to investment phase?
- Large gap between operating CF and net income → check working capital, impairments

### Revenue Structure Analysis
- Concentration risk (dependency on single segment/customer/region)
- Identify high-margin vs. low-margin segments
- Room for mix improvement?

### KPI Assessment
- Are identified KPIs on an improving trend?
- Superior or inferior vs. competitors?
- Consider time lag between KPI improvement and P&L impact
