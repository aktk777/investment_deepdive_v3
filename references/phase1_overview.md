# Phase 1: Overview & Valuation

## Objective
Rapidly grasp the company's full picture and form an initial hypothesis on whether it's undervalued, overvalued, or fairly priced. Prioritize breadth over depth — this hypothesis gets tested in later phases.

## Researcher: Information Gathering

### Items to Search
- Company overview (business description, founding year, headcount, HQ)
- Current stock price, market cap
- PBR (P/B), PER (P/E) — both trailing and forward
- ROA, ROE, ROIC
- PEG ratio (for growth stocks)
- PSR (P/S) (for high-growth or pre-profit companies)
- Audit firm name

### Recommended Search Queries
- `"{company}" PER PBR ROE 2025` / `"{ticker}" valuation metrics`
- `"{company}" 会社概要 事業内容` (for JP stocks)
- `"{company}" auditor` / `"{company}" 監査法人`
- `"{ticker}" market cap`

### Trusted Sources
- Yahoo Finance (JP: finance.yahoo.co.jp / US: finance.yahoo.com)
- Buffett Code, Shikiho Online (JP stocks)
- Company official IR page

## Analyst: Evaluation

### Valuation Comparison
1. **PBR**: Compare to industry average. If PBR < 1.0, investigate why (asset quality? hidden liabilities?)
2. **PER**: Compare to industry & competitors. PER ~10 with growth potential is noteworthy. Extremely high/low PER — identify the reason
3. **ROA / ROE / ROIC**:
   - ROE > 10% is a rough threshold (JP stocks)
   - ROIC > WACC means value creation
   - Large ROA-ROE gap → check leverage impact
4. **PEG Ratio**: PEG < 1.0 suggests undervaluation relative to growth
5. **PSR**: Used for unprofitable SaaS etc. Relative comparison within industry is key

### Audit Firm Check
- Flag if auditor is Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu (トーマツ) — past audit quality concerns
- If non-Big4, assess whether appropriate for company size

### Initial Hypothesis
Form a hypothesis:
- "This stock appears [undervalued/overvalued/fairly priced] because..."
- "Key points to verify in subsequent phases: ..."
