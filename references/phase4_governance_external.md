# Phase 4: Governance & External Evaluation

## Objective
Assess shareholder structure, management quality, and how the market/society perceives the company.
Even great numbers and business models can be undermined by poor governance.
External perspectives compensate for blind spots in your own analysis.

## Researcher: Information Gathering

### Shareholder Structure & Liquidity
- Major shareholder composition (founder, institutional, foreign investors)
- Free float ratio
- Fund flows (new inclusions/exclusions from investment trusts)
- Foreign investor buying/selling trends
- Share buyback and cancellation activity

### Governance
- Board composition (ratio of outside/independent directors)
- Compensation structure (performance-linked?)
- Past scandals or compliance issues
- Parent-subsidiary listing (conflict of interest risk)

### Founder/Management Assessment
- Management background and track record
- Crisis response history (resilience)
- Vision clarity and execution capability
- Stock ownership (skin in the game)

### External Perception
- Analyst consensus rating and target price
- Institutional investor movements (13F filings / 大量保有報告書)
- Employee reviews (OpenWork / Glassdoor)
- Social media sentiment (X/Twitter)
- Customer/user satisfaction

### Recommended Search Queries
- `"{company}" major shareholders ownership structure`
- `"{ticker}" analyst rating target price consensus`
- `"{company}" OpenWork 評判` / `"{company}" Glassdoor reviews`
- `"{ticker}" institutional ownership 13F`
- `"{company}" governance board directors`

### Priority Sources
- Corporate Governance Reports (company website)
- Large shareholding reports (EDINET) / 13F filings (SEC)
- Analyst report summaries from brokerages
- OpenWork / Glassdoor

## Analyst: Evaluation

### Shareholder Structure
- High founder/management ownership → strong alignment
- Increasing foreign investor ratio → positive signal; sharp decline → negative
- Very low float → liquidity risk; very high → hostile takeover risk
- Fund inclusion trends → passive demand shifts

### Governance Quality
- Outside directors ≥ 1/3? (Japan Corporate Governance Code)
- Nomination/compensation committees established?
- Past scandals: severity and quality of response

### Management Assessment
- Evaluate not just successes, but recovery from failures (resilience)
- Balance between vision clarity and execution capability
- Succession planning in place?

### External Perception Cross-Check
- If analyst consensus diverges from your analysis, investigate why
- Poor employee reviews signal organizational sustainability risk
- Strong social sentiment without fundamental backing may indicate hype risk
